# Contributor Code of Conduct

NovaUI contributors and community members are expected to keep project spaces
welcoming, respectful, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discrimination,
intimidation, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private
information without permission.

Please report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the
hosting service's private contact mechanism. This repository does not currently
publish a monitored conduct-reporting address. Maintainers should enable a
private reporting channel before opening public community spaces.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate this policy
and may restrict participation when necessary to protect community members.
